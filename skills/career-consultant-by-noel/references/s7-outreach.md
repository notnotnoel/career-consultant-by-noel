# S7 · Outreach

Covers:
- recruiter replies
- cover emails
- LinkedIn notes
- follow-ups
- thank-you notes

Goal: a message the user would be comfortable sending as their own, sooner rather than later.

## How to write it

- **Write as the user.** Follow the twin's Voice section. If it's empty, default to plain, warm, direct paragraphs with no bullets or bold. A message that reads as AI-written undercuts the candidate before the recruiter reaches the substance.
- **Open with one positioning line** drawn from the S4 priority criterion.
- **Mirror the recruiter's steer.** If they said "focus on your due-diligence work", lead with that.
- **Keep it short:**
  - Recruiter replies: under 150 words.
  - LinkedIn connection notes: two sentences.
  - Follow-ups: three sentences, with one new piece of value, not just "checking in".
  - Thank-you notes: send within 24 hours, and mention one specific thing from the conversation.
- **Offer two variants only when the strategy genuinely differs**, for example "keen and flexible" vs "keen, with a condition on start date". Different wording alone doesn't justify a second version.

## Sending

Never send a message yourself. If an email connector is available, offer to save the message as a draft. The user sends it.

## Tracker

Run `workspace.py track` to set the follow-up action and due date. The default follow-up is 5 business days after the message.

## Standing rules
Use only facts in `00_Career Twin.md` — ask rather than invent a number. A career fact that surfaces here goes into the twin in one line, then stay in this state. Never send, submit, accept or decline anything for the user — draft only. Research people and companies from public professional sources only. Never write ID numbers, bank details or passwords into any file. Before you finish: update the context file (state, what happened, next action, any new rule; keep it under ~150 lines).
