Wide World Importers · Senior Pricing Manager, Asia Pacific
Location: Singapore (hybrid, 3 days in office)

About the role
Wide World Importers distributes industrial and electronic components to 40,000 B2B customers in Asia. You will own list pricing and discount governance for the region, lead a team of 5 pricing analysts, and partner with sales leadership on deal approvals.

What you'll do
- Own the regional price book and annual price-increase cycle across 6 markets
- Run deal-desk governance and discount guardrails with country sales directors
- Build elasticity and price-volume-mix analytics to guide margin decisions
- Present pricing performance to the APAC leadership team monthly
- Lead and develop a team of 5 analysts

Must have
- 7+ years in pricing, revenue management or commercial analytics in B2B
- Proven margin improvement from pricing or discount programmes
- Advanced SQL and Tableau
- Experience leading a team
- Strong stakeholder management with sales leaders

Nice to have
- Mandarin
- Certified Pricing Professional (CPP)
- Experience in industrial or electronics distribution

Salary: S$11,000 – S$13,500 a month plus bonus
