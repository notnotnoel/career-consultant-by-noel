## Build method
1. Copy the master template `.docx`. Replace only the text. Keep all styles, numbering, tables, borders and spacing.
2. Do not create a new document from scratch. Do not change the font, sizes, margins or numbering format.
3. Render to PDF and compare page 1 with the master before delivery.
